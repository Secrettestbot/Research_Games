Informed Consent

Study Purpose

This research investigates decision-making processes and pattern recognition abilities in different contexts.

Procedures

You will be randomly assigned to complete one of three interactive tasks:

  • A clicking game to collect virtual coins
  • A survey about workplace preferences
  • A memory task with playing cards

The task will take approximately 10-15 minutes. You may stop at any time.

Risks and Benefits

Risks: This study involves minimal risk, no more than everyday computer use. You may experience mild fatigue or boredom.

Benefits: You will contribute to scientific understanding of human decision-making. You may also learn about your own decision patterns.

Confidentiality

Your data will be kept strictly confidential. All data will be:

  • Stored securely on encrypted servers
  • Identified only by a random participant code
  • Reported only in aggregate form
  • Retained for up to 7 years per university policy

Voluntary Participation

Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may:

  • Decline to participate without penalty
  • Stop at any time without penalty
  • Skip any questions you prefer not to answer
  • Withdraw your data after participation (within 48 hours)
